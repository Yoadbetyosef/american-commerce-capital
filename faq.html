What is revenue-based financing?
You receive capital upfront and pay it back through a set share of your future revenue, on the schedule in your agreement, until the agreed total is reached.
Can I talk to a real person?
Yes. Capital Specialists are available Monday to Friday, 8am to 7pm ET, at (201) 555-0148.
How much can I qualify for?
It depends on revenue, time in business, and the right structure. ACC provides business capital from $25,000 to $1,000,000. Checking eligibility is the fastest way to know.
How fast is funding?
Decisions come in as little as 4 hours. Once you approve the terms, capital can reach your account as soon as the same day.
What does it cost?
It depends on the structure and the business. Before you accept, you see the total obligation in dollars, every fee, and how payments work. There are no application fees and no hidden fees.
What can I use the funds for?
Any business purpose: inventory, equipment, payroll, expansion or day-to-day cash flow.
What kind of financing is it?
Revenue-based financing and working capital, with terms from 3 to 15 months, built around the revenue of your business.
What if my credit isn't perfect?
We look at how the business performs: revenue, cash flow, time in business. Credit is one factor, not the only one. We never promise approval.
Is ACC the direct funding source?
Yes. ACC underwrites and funds directly. Your application is reviewed by our team, and the capital comes from us. Your file is never sold or passed to a network of lenders.
Will my information be shared or sold?
No. We use it to evaluate your application and communicate with you. You won't hear from other companies because you applied here. See our Privacy Policy for details.
What happens after I apply?
A Capital Specialist reviews your application and contacts you the way you chose. You get the options that may be available, explained. Nothing is funded without your written approval.

Before you apply

Applying is a request for review. Approval is never guaranteed. A Capital Specialist reviews every application personally. Applying does not guarantee approval and does not obligate you to accept funding. Nothing is funded without your written approval.

American Commerce Capital provides commercial financing to established U.S. businesses. Financing is offered as revenue-based financing and working capital with terms of 3 to 15 months. Approval depends on ACC’s funding standards, and final terms, including the funded amount, vary by applicant. Products are for business purposes only and are not consumer loans. All applications are subject to review and approval; approval is not guaranteed. Amounts, costs, terms, and timelines vary and are disclosed in writing before any agreement. ACC products are commercial financing products, not consumer loans, and are structured as business capital agreements. ACC is the direct funder on every transaction. Availability may be subject to state-specific commercial financing disclosure requirements.

American Commerce Capital is a privately held company. It is not a bank and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or backed by the U.S. Small Business Administration or any agency of the United States government.
