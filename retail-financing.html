Financing for retail stores

Retail is a bet on inventory. You buy before you know exactly what will sell, and the shelves have to be full before customers arrive.

ACC helps stores stock up, refresh the space and get through the quiet months.

Why retail stores need working capital

Inventory before the season

Stock is paid for well before it sells.

Uneven months

A few strong months carry the year.

The store itself

Fixtures, displays and repairs add up.

How cash flow works in this business

Sales come in daily, but the biggest costs come in lumps: a seasonal order, a rent payment, a remodel. Suppliers often want payment before or soon after delivery.

A strong season can leave a retailer short on cash right when it is time to order for the next one.

What retail stores finance

Seasonal inventory

Stock for your busiest months.

New product lines

Testing something new.

Store improvements

Fixtures, displays and signage.

A second location

Lease, build-out and opening stock.

Marketing

Bringing customers through the door.

Point-of-sale systems

Hardware and software.

When financing makes sense

A good fit, and a poor one.

A good fit

  • Consistent revenue in the business account
  • A clear gap between costs and payment
  • A specific use for the funds
  • Work or orders already lined up
  • The capital will earn more than it costs

A poor fit

  • Covering losses instead of timing
  • Revenue that is falling
  • No plan for how the funds will be used
  • Several existing advances already in place
  • A need that can wait for lower-cost financing
