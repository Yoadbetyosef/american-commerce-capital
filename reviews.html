American Commerce Capital provides commercial financing to established U.S. businesses. Financing is offered as revenue-based financing and working capital with terms of 3 to 15 months. Approval depends on ACC’s funding standards, and final terms, including the funded amount, vary by applicant. Products are for business purposes only and are not consumer loans. All applications are subject to review and approval; approval is not guaranteed. Amounts, costs, terms, and timelines vary and are disclosed in writing before any agreement. ACC products are commercial financing products, not consumer loans, and are structured as business capital agreements. ACC is the direct funder on every transaction.
