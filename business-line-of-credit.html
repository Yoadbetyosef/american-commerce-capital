What is a business line of credit?

A business line of credit gives your company access to a set limit. You draw only what you need, when you need it, and you pay for the amount you actually use.

As you pay it down, the available amount opens up again. It suits owners who face recurring, uneven costs and want capital on standby instead of a single lump sum.

Benefits of a business line of credit

Use it when you need it

Capital is there for the slow week, the big order or the surprise repair.

Pay for what you draw

Cost applies to the amount in use, not to the full limit.

It refills

Pay it down and the available amount is ready again.

Any business purpose

Payroll, inventory, supplies or a gap between invoices.

How does a business line of credit work?

You apply once and, if approved, receive a limit sized to your revenue and bank activity. When you need funds you request a draw, and the money is sent to your business bank account.

Each draw is paid back on the schedule in your agreement. As the balance comes down, that amount becomes available to draw again without a new application.

What a line of credit costs.

Cost on a line is tied to what you draw and how long you keep it. A limit you do not touch is not the same as money you owe.

Before you take a draw, ACC shows you the total you will pay back and the payment schedule for that draw.

Line of credit or working capital loan?

Both put capital to work. Where they differ is how you receive it and how you pay it back.

Is it the right choice?

An honest look at both sides. If another product fits you better, we will tell you.

It makes sense when

  • Your costs come in waves
  • You want capital on standby
  • You do not need it all at once
  • You bridge gaps between invoices
  • You want to reuse it without reapplying

It is the wrong choice when

  • You have one large purchase to make
  • You want a single fixed payment
  • You would draw the full limit and keep it
  • Revenue is too irregular to support draws
  • You need long terms for a big project

Terms to understand

The words you will see in an offer, in plain English.

Credit limit

The most you can have drawn at one time.

Draw

An amount you take from the line.

Available credit

The limit minus what you currently owe.

Draw period

The time during which you can take draws.

Payment schedule

How often each draw is paid back.

Total payback

What you pay in full for a given draw.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a line of credit different from a loan?
A loan gives you one lump sum. A line lets you draw as you go, up to your limit, and reuse it as you pay it down.
Do I pay for the part I do not use?
Your cost is tied to the amount you draw. Your agreement lists any other fees before you sign.
How large can my limit be?
ACC funds from $25,000 to $1,000,000, sized to your revenue and bank activity.
How fast can I draw?
Decisions come in as little as 4 hours and funding can arrive as soon as the same day. Timing depends on your file.
Can I draw more than once?
Yes. As you pay down the balance, that amount becomes available again.
What can I use it for?
Any business purpose.
