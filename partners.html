Partner questions

Good to know.

How much commission do I earn?

8% to 15% of the funded amount, set in your partner agreement and shown on every deal in your portal.

When is my commission earned?

When the deal funds. You do not wait for the merchant's payments to start.

What deals do you fund?

Merchant cash advances from $25,000 to $1,000,000 for businesses in every state except Vermont. No credit check, no collateral and no personal guarantee. Terms depend on each business's revenue and bank activity.

Will you contact my merchants directly?

Only about the deal you submitted. Deals you bring are tied to you, and we do not market to your merchants without your permission.

Who can become a partner?

ISOs, brokers, referral partners, CPAs and advisors who work with small business owners. Every application is reviewed, and each partner signs an agreement before submitting deals.
