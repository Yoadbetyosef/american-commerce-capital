What is a merchant cash advance?

With a merchant cash advance, your business receives a lump sum today. In return, the funder buys a set amount of your future sales, which you pay back through regular payments taken from your revenue.

Because it is based on what your business brings in, the review looks first at your sales and bank activity. It fits businesses with steady revenue that would rather move quickly.

How does a merchant cash advance work?

Qualifying is simple: a short application and recent business bank statements. ACC reviews your sales and deposits, then comes back with an amount, the total you pay back and the payment schedule.

Accept the offer and funds are sent to your business bank account. From there, payments run on their own until the agreed total is paid.

How the cost works.

The cost of a merchant cash advance is usually expressed as a factor rate, not an interest rate. Multiply the advance by the factor rate and you get the total you pay back. That total is fixed on the day you sign.

A factor rate is not an APR, so it cannot be compared directly with a bank interest rate. What matters is the total payback in dollars and how long it takes. ACC shows you both before you accept anything. If a lower-cost product fits you, we will say so.

Is it the right choice?

An honest look at both sides. If another product fits you better, we will tell you.

It makes sense when

  • You need capital in days, not weeks
  • Your sales are steady and easy to show
  • The money will earn more than it costs
  • A bank has said no or is too slow
  • You want payments tied to your revenue

It is the wrong choice when

  • You can wait for lower-cost financing
  • Your sales are irregular or falling
  • You are covering ongoing losses
  • You already carry several advances
  • You have no clear plan for the funds

Terms to understand

The words you will see in an offer, in plain English.

Advance amount

The lump sum sent to your business.

Factor rate

The number that sets the total you pay back.

Total payback

The advance multiplied by the factor rate.

Remittance

The regular payment: fixed, or a share of sales.

Payment schedule

How often payments are collected, such as daily or weekly.

Reconciliation

A review of payments against actual sales, where your agreement provides for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a merchant cash advance a loan?
No. It is a purchase of a set amount of your future sales. It has a total payback amount instead of an interest rate.
How is the amount decided?
It is sized to your revenue, time in business and bank activity. ACC funds from $25,000 to $1,000,000.
How do payments work?
Payments are collected automatically on the schedule in your agreement, as a fixed amount or as a share of sales.
What if my sales drop?
Call your Capital Specialist. If your agreement ties payments to a share of sales, they move with your revenue. Either way, talk to us early.
Can I pay it off early?
You can pay the remaining balance at any time. Your agreement explains exactly how early payoff works.
How fast is funding?
Decisions come in as little as 4 hours and funding can arrive as soon as the same day. Timing depends on your file.
